## Why does water still collect near my Katy or Cypress home after gutter work?

Start with the whole path: roof → gutter → downspout → discharge point. Clear gutters cannot solve every yard or street-drainage problem. Ask the provider to explain where the roof water will go and whether the proposed work also addresses the discharge point. Public drainage and private roof drainage are different systems; a gutter quote should be specific about which problem it addresses.

Source basis: NC State Extension's gutter-management guidance discusses gutters, clear downspouts and discharge away from structures. Houston Public Works describes public drainage rehabilitation, including storm sewers, inlets and ditches. This is editorial application to Katy/Cypress readers, not a claim that Houston city services cover every Katy/Cypress address.
- https://content.ces.ncsu.edu/moisture-control-and-prevention-gutter-management
- https://www.houstonpublicworks.org/drainage-rehabilitation

## What should I include when requesting a gutter estimate in West Houston?

Describe whether you need installation, guards or a repair. Note which part of the home is affected, whether water overflows during rain, and what you can see safely from the ground. Ask for an itemized scope covering materials, gutter runs, downspouts, removal and disposal, access needs, and any maintenance the proposed guards still require. Compare the scope of work before comparing totals. No universal local price is quoted here because no current local estimates have been collected.

This is an original quote-comparison checklist, not a sourced price forecast or contractor endorsement.

## Will gutter guards make the system maintenance-free?

No. Ask how the proposed guard handles your roof runoff and nearby debris, how the system will be inspected, and which cleaning tasks remain. Guards do not remove the need to check gutters and downspouts.

Source basis: Texas General Land Office coastal stormwater guidance includes routine checks of gutters and guards in rainwater-harvesting systems. Its context is maintenance, not endorsement of a particular guard product.
- https://www.glo.texas.gov/sites/default/files/coastal-grants/_documents/grant-project/ciap-2008-coastal-storm-water-technical%20guidance.pdf
